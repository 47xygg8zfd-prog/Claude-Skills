# Case Study: From Low Engagement to Shipped Feature

A complete end-to-end PM case study showing how the skills, workflows, templates, and metrics in this repo work together on a single realistic scenario.

**Product**: Pulse — B2B team analytics for engineering managers  
**Problem**: 68% of users log in fewer than twice per week  
**Outcome**: Weekly digest feature shipped in Q3; WAU up 12 points in 8 weeks
