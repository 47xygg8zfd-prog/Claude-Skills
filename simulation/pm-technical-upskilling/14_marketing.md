# Launch Marketing Brief: TechBridge — PM Technical Fluency Platform
**Stage**: Product Marketing | **Date**: 2026-05-12

---

## Positioning

**One-sentence position**:
TechBridge is the contextual learning tool that B2B SaaS product managers use to understand technical conversations in the moment, unlike coding courses that teach programming PMs will never use.

**Target customer**: Mid-level PM (2–5 years) at a B2B SaaS company with a dedicated engineering team. Has enough experience to know what they don't know. Doesn't need to code — needs to *participate*.

**Their pain today**: They're Googling technical terms during architecture reviews, asking their tech lead to explain things after the meeting, and staying silent in sprint planning because they don't have a framework for evaluating estimates. It erodes their credibility and their relationship with the team.

**The transformation**:
- Before: "I nodded along and then Slacked my tech lead afterward to find out what 'idempotent' actually means"
- After: "I pasted the design doc excerpt before the meeting and walked in with three questions worth asking"

TechBridge is built for the product manager who's technically curious but not technical. Paste any Slack message, ticket, or design doc excerpt and get a plain-language explanation written for how you actually work — with the context to ask better questions, push back on estimates, and write requirements engineers trust. Not a coding course. Not a bootcamp. Just the technical fluency you need for the job you have.

**CTA**: Start explaining for free

**Proof points**:
- Explanations in 3 sections: plain English, why engineers care, what to ask next
- 50+ PM-specific concepts explained in workflow context (not developer documentation)
- Step-by-step guides for sprint estimation, architecture reviews, incident debriefs, technical debt triage, and writing technical requirements
- Mobile-first — works in the 60 seconds before a meeting

## What Not to Say

- "AI-powered learning platform" — sounds like every other EdTech product; buries the specific use case
- "Improve your technical skills" — vague; we're not building a skills certification; PMs don't want "skills," they want to stop feeling embarrassed
- "Bridge the gap between product and engineering" — overused; means nothing specific
- "Comprehensive technical curriculum" — we are explicitly NOT a curriculum; this is the opposite of our positioning
- "Seamless", "robust", "best-in-class" — banned per messaging guidelines

---

## GTM Risks

1. **Freemium conversion**: If PMs pay personally, the price ceiling is ~$10–15/month. We need a clear trigger for the paywall (after N explanations, or after day 7). Do not ship without a monetization hypothesis. Owner: CEO + PM. Target: before launch.
2. **Content quality on launch day**: If the concept library has fewer than 30 high-quality entries, early users will judge the product as thin. Minimum viable content: 50 concepts + 3 workflow guides. Do not launch without hitting this bar.
3. **SEO from blog content**: The blog post titles above are designed to capture search traffic from PMs actively looking for help with technical conversations. Publishing at least 2 posts before launch builds organic discovery before paid spend begins.
