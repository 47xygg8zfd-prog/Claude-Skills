# Decision Log: [Product / Team Name]

A running record of significant product and technical decisions. Add a new entry each time a meaningful decision is made.

---

## How to Use

Add entries in reverse chronological order (newest at top). A decision is worth logging if:
- It involves a real tradeoff
- It would be confusing to a future team member without context
- It's likely to be revisited or questioned later
- It involves scope, architecture, pricing, or customer-facing behavior
