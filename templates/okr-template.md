## Scoring Guide

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 1.0 | Exceeded target — set the bar too low next time |
| 0.7 | Hit target — this is success |
| 0.5 | Made meaningful progress but fell short |
| 0.3 | Started but significantly missed |
| 0.0 | No progress |

> A consistent score of 1.0 means your targets are too easy. Aim for 0.6–0.7 on average.
