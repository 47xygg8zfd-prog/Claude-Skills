## Status Summary

Three bullets. What shipped, what's blocked, what changed. No more.

- **Shipped**: [What went out this week — link to release notes or Jira ticket if relevant]
- **Blocked**: [What's stuck and why — name the blocker, not the symptom]
- **Changed**: [What's different from last week's plan — scope, timeline, priority, direction]

If nothing changed, say so. "No changes to plan" is an acceptable entry and builds trust with stakeholders.
