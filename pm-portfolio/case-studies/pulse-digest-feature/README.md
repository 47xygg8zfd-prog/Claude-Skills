# Pulse Weekly Digest Email — PM Case Study

## Feature Overview

The weekly digest email is a Monday-morning summary sent to engineering managers that surfaces their team's most important signals from the past week: sprint predictability trend, top open PRs by age, any members with blocked or idle time, and one prioritized recommendation from Pulse. It is the primary engagement surface in the product and the foundation of our north star metric, digest-active WAU.

## The Problem It Solved

Engineering managers at mid-market SaaS companies have no shortage of dashboards. What they lack is a reliable forcing function to act on team health data weekly — before a sprint slips, not after. Pulse existed in-app, but WAU was low because managers had no strong reason to return without a prompt. The digest gave them one.

## My Role

I led the feature from discovery through rollout as the sole PM on a three-person squad (1 FE, 1 BE, 1 designer). I owned problem framing, the PRD, experiment design, stakeholder alignment, and the post-launch retro.

## Key Outcomes

| Metric | Baseline | Target | Achieved |
|--------|----------|--------|----------|
| Weekly digest open rate | 61% | 75% | 73% |
| Digest-active WAU | 38% | 52% | 49% |
| 30-day retention | 64% | 78% | 76% |
| Time-to-first-insight | 8 days | 3 days | 3.2 days |

The feature shipped to 100% of accounts after a successful A/B test. Within six weeks of full rollout, Pulse saw its highest NPS quarter since launch.
