# Product Teardowns

A product teardown is a structured competitive analysis — not a feature list, but a PM-level read of why a product exists, who it's built for, and where it's vulnerable. Good teardowns make you a better builder. They force you to ask uncomfortable questions: why did they make that tradeoff? what does their retention loop assume about user behavior? where is the wedge a competitor could drive?

Each teardown follows the same 13-section framework:

1. **TL;DR** — the product in one sentence
2. **What It's Really Optimizing For** — the hidden objective beneath the stated pitch
3. **Key Metrics & What They Reveal** — which numbers they care about and what that tells you about the business
4. **Jobs to Be Done** — functional, emotional, and social JTBD
5. **Target Segment** — who specifically, and what they need
6. **Onboarding & Aha Moment** — how they get users to value fast
7. **Growth Loop** — the self-reinforcing engine that drives acquisition or expansion
8. **Retention Mechanics** — what keeps users coming back
9. **Monetization & Alignment** — whether pricing reinforces or fights the value proposition
10. **Feature Strategy** — what they've built and what it signals about their bets
11. **Weaknesses & Vulnerabilities** — where a competitor could win
12. **3 Lessons for Any PM** — transferable takeaways regardless of industry
13. **If I Were PM Here** — what I'd build next and why
