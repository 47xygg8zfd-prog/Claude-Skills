# Example Output: Competitive Analysis Skill

**Prompt used:** "Write a competitor teardown and battlecard for Teamlytics, our main competitor in the manager analytics space"

---

## Competitor Teardown: Teamlytics

**Threat Level**: High

**Positioning**  
"The all-in-one people analytics platform for modern engineering teams." Leads with breadth — covers everything from sprint metrics to 1:1 tracking to compensation benchmarking.

**Target Customer**  
Mid-market to enterprise engineering orgs (100–2,000 engineers). Strong in companies that have a dedicated People Analytics or HR Ops function to own the tool. Less common as a PM-owned purchase.

**Key Strengths**
1. **Breadth of data**: Integrates with 40+ tools (Jira, GitHub, Slack, Workday, BambooHR). Pulse integrates with 12.
2. **Executive reporting**: Pre-built board-ready dashboards. Strong top-down purchase motion.
3. **Established brand**: 5 years in market, 800+ customers, visible on G2 and Gartner Peer Insights.

**Key Weaknesses**
1. **Complexity**: Average time-to-value is 6–8 weeks (per their own case studies). Pulse is 3 days.
2. **Manager experience neglected**: Built for HR and executives, not frontline managers. Manager NPS in G2 reviews averages 3.6/5 vs. Pulse's 4.4/5.
3. **Pricing**: Starts at $35/seat/month (vs. Pulse at $18/seat/month). Hard sell for budget-conscious mid-market buyers.

**Pricing**
- $35/seat/month (annual contract required)
- Minimum 50 seats (~$21k/year floor)
- Professional Services for implementation: $8–15k typical

**Recent Moves**
- Raised $40M Series C in February 2026 — expect increased sales headcount and marketing spend H2 2026
- Launched "Manager Digest" feature in April 2026 (beta) — directly competitive with our digest
- Hiring aggressively for enterprise AEs (10 open roles on LinkedIn)

## Sales Battlecard: Teamlytics

**When you'll face them**  
- Deals with 200+ engineers
- Prospects who mention "people analytics" or "HR data"
- Competitive RFPs that include Workday/BambooHR integration requirements

**Their pitch**  
"We're the most complete people analytics platform. If you want to understand your engineering org end-to-end — from sprint velocity to attrition risk — we have it all in one place."

**Our counter**  
"Complete is great if you have a team to manage it. Teamlytics takes 6–8 weeks to implement and requires a dedicated admin. Pulse is live in 3 days, and managers actually use it — our WAU is 3x higher in head-to-head deployments. You don't need every integration on day one. You need the right insights, fast."

**Landmines to plant**  
- *"How long did implementation take for reference customers like yours?"* (Teamlytics is notoriously slow)
- *"Who in your org will own this day-to-day?"* (They need an admin; Pulse doesn't)
- *"Have you talked to frontline managers, or just the executives sponsoring the deal?"* (Teamlytics scores poorly with actual managers)

**Proof Points**
- "We replaced Teamlytics at [Customer]. Onboarding went from 8 weeks to 4 days, and manager login frequency tripled in the first month." — Head of Engineering, Series B SaaS
- Pulse manager NPS: +44. Teamlytics manager NPS (G2): +12.
- 94% of Pulse customers go live within 5 business days.

**When we lose**  
Be honest: Teamlytics wins when the buyer needs deep HR system integration (Workday, BambooHR) or when the primary user is a People Analytics team, not managers. If the deal is really about attrition modeling or compensation benchmarking, Teamlytics is a better fit — and it's better to say so than to oversell.
