## The Pulse Story

All examples follow the Pulse product team through a single quarter:

1. **Research** uncovers why managers don't log in → `customer-research-synthesis`
2. **Prioritization** puts digest and SSO at the top of Q3 → `feature-prioritization`
3. **OKRs** set targets for WAU, onboarding, and retention → `okrs`
4. **PRD** scopes the digest feature → `prd`
5. **Stories** break it into sprint tickets → `agile-stories`
6. **Retro** reviews what went well and what didn't → `agile-ceremonies`
7. **Monte Carlo** forecasts the launch date → `monte-carlo`
8. **Tech Translation** decodes engineering concerns → `tech-translation`
9. **Data Queries** measure WAU and delivery errors → `data-queries`
10. **Dashboards** visualize digest adoption → `quicksight-dashboards`
11. **Release Notes** communicate what shipped → `release-notes`
12. **Stakeholder Updates** keep execs and finance in the loop → `stakeholder-updates`
13. **Competitive Analysis** arms sales against the main competitor → `competitive-analysis`
14. **Presentation** delivers the mid-quarter exec update → `pm-presentations`
15. **Automation** scopes a Jira → PRD workflow for next quarter → `claude-automation`
