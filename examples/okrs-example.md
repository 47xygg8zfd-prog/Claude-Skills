### Check-in Narrative

Onboarding improvements are tracking well and are the clearest bright spot this quarter. Engagement KRs (WAU, login frequency) are at risk primarily because the weekly digest — our highest-confidence intervention — hasn't launched yet. Digest goes live in Week 8; if it hits its open rate target, the engagement KRs should recover by end of quarter. Retention KRs naturally lag engagement by 90 days, so the current scores understate progress; we'll have a clearer read in Q4. Recommend holding the current plan and re-evaluating engagement KRs after two weeks of digest data.
